FEATURE PROMPT — COMPLETE OPEN WORLD MAP

Design an original connected open world with three large fictional cities and a surrounding countryside. Give each city a unique architectural language, culture, skyline, road network, landmarks, and gameplay identity.

CITY A — COASTAL METROPOLIS
Downtown towers, marina, beaches, luxury residences, shopping district, nightlife, port, airport, highway loop, waterfront park, hotels, restaurants, and residential neighborhoods.

CITY B — INDUSTRIAL / FINANCIAL CITY
Financial district, banks, warehouses, factories, freight yard, dense neighborhoods, stadium, university, hospitals, police HQ, fire stations, and underground parking.

CITY C — HILLSIDE / CULTURAL CITY
Old town, modern suburbs, hillside roads, large botanical garden, museums, markets, rail station, lakefront, mountain access, and scenic viewpoints.

COUNTRYSIDE
Villages, farms, forests, rivers, lakes, waterfalls, hills, caves only if safely designed, rural roads, campgrounds, wildlife zones, and small service towns.

MAP REQUIREMENTS
- Establish world scale using travel-time targets and gameplay density, not arbitrary square-kilometer claims.
- Create a readable world map, minimap, district names, roads, paths, points of interest, mission markers, owned properties, shops, and service locations.
- Add discoverable landmarks and optional collectibles.
- Ensure roads connect logically; add bridges, tunnels, intersections, traffic lights, sidewalks, and pedestrian crossings.
- Each district needs a gameplay purpose and visual identity.
- Include accessible routes and clear navigation cues.
- Define which buildings are enterable, which have interiors, and which are exterior-only.
- Use streaming and optimized detail; do not keep every district fully loaded.

DELIVERABLES
World map document, district list, landmark list, road hierarchy, streaming-cell plan, interior list, navigation design, and performance budget. Do not use a map copied from a commercial game.
