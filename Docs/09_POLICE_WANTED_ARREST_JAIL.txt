FEATURE PROMPT — POLICE, WANTED LEVEL, PURSUIT AND JAIL

Create a reactive fictional law-enforcement system.

CRIME EVENTS
Gameplay systems emit structured events when defined illegal actions occur. Each event has severity, location, witness visibility, evidence, and timestamp. Do not infer crimes from arbitrary player movement.

DETECTION
NPC witnesses can notice events within perception limits and report them. Cameras or alarms can be mission-specific. The system must be fair, explainable, and consistent.

WANTED SYSTEM
Use multiple escalation levels with clear UI feedback. Higher levels can trigger additional patrols, roadblocks, specialist units, search patterns, and air support only when appropriate to the game's scale.

POLICE AI
Patrol routes, investigate reports, search last known position, pursue vehicles, coordinate backup, use safe navigation, and disengage when search conditions are met. Avoid police teleporting directly beside the player.

ARREST
Police can stop and arrest the player when conditions are met. Add surrender/escape rules, arrest animation, transition to jail or processing, fines or item confiscation rules, and return-to-world flow.

JAIL
Create a playable or short interactive jail sequence, release rules, and persistent consequences. Do not trap the player in an unrecoverable state.

QA
Test line of sight, witness reporting, wanted escalation, pursuit navigation, vehicle chase, search timeout, arrest, jail release, save/load, and edge cases.
