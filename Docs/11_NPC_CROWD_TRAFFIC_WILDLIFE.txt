FEATURE PROMPT — LIVING WORLD AI

Create believable but performance-aware NPC systems.

PEDESTRIANS
Spawn by district and time of day. Support walking routes, crossing roads, sitting, shopping, conversations, reacting to events, fleeing danger, calling emergency services, and despawning safely outside relevant simulation ranges.

TRAFFIC
Use lane and road graph data. Vehicles obey traffic signals and basic right-of-way, avoid collisions, stop for pedestrians, react to emergency vehicles, and use district-specific traffic density. Prevent gridlock and impossible lane changes.

WILDLIFE
Add selected animals appropriate to each biome, with roaming, idle, flee, and ambient behavior. Avoid overpopulating areas.

SCHEDULES
Use lightweight time-of-day activity profiles. Simulate distant NPCs cheaply and activate detailed behavior near the player.

PERFORMANCE
Use spawn budgets, pooling, distance-based AI updates, significance systems, and safe streaming. Measure CPU, memory, and frame time.

QA
Test crowds in dense districts, traffic intersections, emergency response, streaming boundaries, save/load, and low-end mobile performance.
