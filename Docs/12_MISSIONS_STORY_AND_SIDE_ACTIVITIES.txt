FEATURE PROMPT — MAIN STORY, SIDE MISSIONS AND ACTIVITIES

Write an original story with new characters, locations, dialogue, and fictional organizations. Do not imitate copyrighted GTA characters, missions, dialogue, or plotlines.

MISSION TYPES
Main story missions, side quests, delivery jobs, taxi work, rescue activities, races, driving challenges, collectibles, photography, property tasks, and emergent world events.

MISSION DATA
Each mission must define:
- Unique ID and title.
- Narrative summary.
- Start location and prerequisites.
- Objectives and optional objectives.
- Checkpoints.
- Fail conditions and restart behavior.
- NPC/vehicle/world dependencies.
- Rewards and unlocks.
- Save/resume behavior.
- Localization and subtitles.
- QA test cases.

FREE ROAM
The player must be able to ignore missions and explore. Avoid forced mission interruptions except for clearly communicated story sequences.

MISSION UI
Show objective, waypoint, distance, progress, pause/restart options, and accessibility settings. Use map markers consistently.

QA
Test mission start, completion, failure, checkpoint, player death/arrest, world streaming, save/load, and replay prevention.
