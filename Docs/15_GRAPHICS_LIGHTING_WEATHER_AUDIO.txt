FEATURE PROMPT — REALISTIC VISUALS, WEATHER AND AUDIO

VISUAL TARGET
Create a high-fidelity original world using physically based materials, realistic scale, coherent art direction, detailed environments, believable vegetation, vehicle materials, interiors, lighting, and animation.

LIGHTING
Use the selected Unreal rendering features after testing on target platforms. PC may use higher-end lighting and effects; Android must use a supported scalable rendering path. Do not assume every UE5 desktop feature works on every phone.

WEATHER
Clear, cloudy, rain, fog, storms where appropriate, wet surfaces, puddles, wind, and weather transitions. Weather must affect visuals and ambience; only add gameplay effects if they are tested and fair.

DAY/NIGHT
Use a consistent time system with sunrise, sunset, night lighting, traffic light schedules, shop schedules, and ambient audio transitions.

AUDIO
Original or licensed music, city ambience, interiors, footsteps, vehicles, weather, UI, NPC voice, police sirens, and spatial audio. Track every audio license. Do not use unlicensed commercial game music.

OPTIMIZATION
LOD/HLOD, culling, texture streaming, light budgets, shadow distance, scalable effects, and profiling. Provide mobile fallback assets and settings.

QA
Capture comparable test scenes and measure frame time, memory, shader compilation, audio transitions, and thermal behavior on real devices.
