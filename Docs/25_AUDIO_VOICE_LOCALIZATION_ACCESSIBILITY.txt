PROMPT — AUDIO, VOICE, LANGUAGE AND ACCESSIBILITY

Create an original soundscape and inclusive UI.

AUDIO
Footsteps, clothing movement, traffic, engines, horns, brakes, doors, city ambience, nature, weather, interiors, mission cues, police sirens, UI sounds, and original music. Use spatial audio where supported.

VOICE
Original character dialogue and NPC barks. Use licensed or commissioned voice talent, or clearly labeled temporary recordings during development. Do not imitate a real actor without permission.

LANGUAGES
Plan English and Hindi first if the project team can support accurate localization. Keep all text in localization tables, support subtitles, and avoid hard-coded strings.

ACCESSIBILITY
Subtitle size/background, color contrast, remappable controls, adjustable sensitivity, camera shake toggle, motion blur toggle, readable UI, audio sliders, and color-independent alerts.

QA
Check audio mix, subtitle timing, localization overflow, text readability on small phones, and accessibility settings persistence.
