DETAILED IMPLEMENTATION PROMPT — HUMAN NPC BEHAVIOR AND SOCIAL AI

Create believable fictional pedestrians and residents. AI must be robust, performance-aware, and designed for game simulation.

NPC ARCHETYPES
Residents, commuters, shoppers, workers, tourists, students, security staff, emergency responders, service workers, and mission characters. Each archetype has a data-driven schedule and behavior profile.

DAILY SCHEDULE
Define time-of-day routines such as leaving home, commuting, working, eating, shopping, socializing, exercising, and returning home. Use simplified simulation for distant NPCs and detailed behavior near the player.

INTERACTIONS
NPCs can greet, respond to contextual dialogue, use shops, sit, walk, cross roads, react to weather, notice disturbances, seek safety, call emergency services, and resume normal routines when safe.

PERCEPTION
Use bounded sight, hearing, and event perception. Avoid omniscient AI. NPC reactions should depend on distance, line of sight, sound level, context, and personality profile.

DIALOGUE
Use original short dialogue lines with localization support, subtitles, cooldowns, and non-repetitive selection. Do not imitate dialogue from existing commercial games or actors.

TECHNICAL
Use navigation meshes or verified navigation systems, behavior trees/state trees, smart objects, animation interfaces, pooling, distance-based updates, and spawn budgets. Prevent NPCs from walking through walls, getting stuck indefinitely, or spawning visibly in front of the player.

QA
Test crowded areas, narrow interiors, moving vehicles, weather changes, emergency events, streaming boundaries, and low-end device performance. Track CPU cost and AI update rates.
