DETAILED IMPLEMENTATION PROMPT — WATER, OCEAN, RIVERS AND SWIMMING

Create visually convincing and technically scalable water environments.

WATER TYPES
Ocean, beach shallows, lakes, rivers, ponds, fountains, waterfalls, and swimming pools. Each water body must have correct boundaries, collision, audio zones, and visual transitions.

VISUALS
Use reflections, waves, shoreline foam, underwater color and visibility, wet surfaces, and water interaction effects where supported. Provide lower-cost mobile rendering options.

PLAYER INTERACTION
Implement swimming only if the character animations, stamina/oxygen rules if any, camera, entry/exit transitions, and collision are complete. Define safe shore exits and prevent trapping the player underwater.

VEHICLES
Boats may be added with water buoyancy and steering only after a functional prototype is integrated and profiled. Do not advertise watercraft before controls and physics work.

WORLD INTEGRATION
Connect rivers and coastlines to terrain and road bridges. Ensure bridges, docks, beaches, and shore paths have valid collision and navigation.

QA
Test water entry/exit, shoreline transitions, rain, reflections, swimming controls, mobile performance, and save/load near water.
