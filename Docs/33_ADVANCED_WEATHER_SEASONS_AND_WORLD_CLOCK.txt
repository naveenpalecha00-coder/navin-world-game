DETAILED IMPLEMENTATION PROMPT — WEATHER, SEASONS AND WORLD CLOCK

Build a coherent world clock and weather simulation.

WORLD CLOCK
Use a configurable day length, sunrise/sunset, lighting transitions, shop schedules, traffic density profiles, NPC routines, and mission time conditions. Provide a time display only if it is part of the intended UI.

WEATHER TYPES
Clear, overcast, light rain, heavy rain, fog, wind, and storms where supported. Seasonal variation may change foliage, lighting, ambient audio, and selected world dressing.

TRANSITIONS
Weather should transition smoothly using authored or data-driven curves. Avoid abrupt lighting or material changes.

GAMEPLAY EFFECTS
Only add wet-road traction, visibility changes, or NPC behavior changes when they are tuned, readable, and tested. Weather should not randomly make controls unfair.

PERFORMANCE
Use scalable particle counts, effects distance, shadow settings, reflection quality, and mobile fallbacks. Avoid expensive effects on devices that cannot sustain them.

QA
Test time transitions, weather changes, interiors, vehicle surfaces, water, mission triggers, and device thermal behavior.
