DETAILED IMPLEMENTATION PROMPT — SHOPS, SERVICES AND LEGAL JOBS

Create meaningful legal activities and city services that make the world feel alive.

SHOPS
Clothing stores, food shops, convenience stores, vehicle dealerships, repair garages, electronics shops, and selected specialty stores. Every shop needs a real interaction and purchase flow before being listed as functional.

SERVICES
Taxi, vehicle repair, hospital treatment, property services, garage storage, public transport if implemented, and emergency services.

LEGAL JOBS
Delivery, taxi driving, courier work, maintenance tasks, rescue/service activities, and other original jobs. Each job needs start conditions, objective flow, reward, fail rules, and replay cooldown where appropriate.

ECONOMY INTEGRATION
All purchases and rewards must use the same validated economy system. Prevent duplicate charges, negative balances, and duplicate rewards.

QA
Test insufficient funds, inventory capacity, shop opening hours, mission interruptions, save/load, and mobile/PC controls.
